# Getting started

*The four steps from a meeting to a set of minutes*

HeyPia records a meeting, transcribes it to text here on your machine, and
turns the text into a document if you ask it to.

## 1. Record

Press **Record meeting** at the top left when the meeting begins. You name it
afterwards — it should not have to be decided while people are saying hello.

If it is a webinar where you only listen, press **Webinar** instead. Then only
what the computer plays is recorded, and the recording takes half the space
and half the time.

The shortcut key works whatever program you are in. It is shown at the top
right, next to the record button.

## 2. Transcribe

Go to **Recordings**, select the recording, and press **Create transcript**.

It happens on your machine. The audio does not leave it. With an NVIDIA
graphics card it runs faster than the audio lasts; without one it takes longer
than the meeting itself.

You can safely do something else meanwhile — including recording a new
meeting.

## 3. Correct what was misheard

The text will not be perfect. Names and technical terms are what most often
come out wrong.

Click into a line and type. The correction is stored separately, so what the
machine heard is not lost — and it is the corrected text that documents and
search use.

## 4. Make the document

Press **Create document** and choose a meeting type: minutes, a task list, or
one you have made yourself.

That is the one step where the text is sent out of the house — to a language
model in Europe. The audio stays put. Read more under **Where your data
goes**.

## Always say that you are recording

It is good practice, and for online meetings it is necessary: the meeting
program gives no notice when the audio is recorded this way.

You can say more than that you are recording. You can say where the recording
ends up: it stays on your machine.
