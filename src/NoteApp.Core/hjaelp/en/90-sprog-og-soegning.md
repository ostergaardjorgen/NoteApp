# Language and search

*The flag at the top right — and how to find something that was said*

## Change language

Click the flag at the top right. The app switches straight away; no restart is
needed.

Dates and weekdays follow. «Thursday 27 August» is as much English as
«Recordings».

**The language of the app is not the language of the meeting.** Which language
you and the guests *speak* is chosen when a recording is transcribed. You can
happily run the app in English and hold your meetings in Danish.

## Search

The search field in the Cockpit looks through **everything said and written**:
all transcripts, the notes you wrote as you went, and the documents the app
has made.

More words means they must all appear close together.

The files are read on your machine. Nothing is sent.

Below the field you can filter by period, folder, meeting type and language.
The tabs split the results by type, because the three answer different things:
what was **said**, what **you** wrote down, and what was **written**.

A meeting can only be searched once it has been transcribed. A recording on
its own holds no words to look through.

## Folders

A recording can be put in a folder — a client, a project. Drag it onto the
folder in the tree, or use **Move to folder**.

It is a field, not a real folder on disk. That is why a recording can be moved
without documents that point to it losing track of it.

## Archive

A meeting that is completely finished can be dragged down into **Archive**.
That keeps the list at the front showing only what still needs something.

The archive is not a wastebasket. Nothing is deleted.
