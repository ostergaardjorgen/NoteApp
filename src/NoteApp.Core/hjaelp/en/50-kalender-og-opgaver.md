# Calendar and tasks

*Today's meetings in the Cockpit — and what Google has to do with it*

The calendar on the left of the Cockpit shows what is happening today and over
the coming days. Click an event to open it.

The calendar works **without an account anywhere**. It is not a stopgap: the
app's own calendar is the real calendar, and you can always create events
here.

## Automatic recording

Open an event and tick **Start the recording automatically**. Then it begins
two minutes before.

It requires **HeyPia to be running**. A closed program cannot start a
recording, and that is not something that can be changed — not even with a
scheduled task in Windows, because the first seconds would go on loading the
program.

If the meeting moves in the calendar, the recording moves with it.

You can also set meeting type, folder and language on the event. Then nothing
has to be answered while the meeting is starting.

## Google Calendar

Under **Settings → Integrations** you can connect Google Calendar. Then your
events are read in, and you can press record directly on a meeting.

**One scope** is requested: your events. Not the mail, not the files, not the
contacts. The approval happens in your own browser at Google.

**Only fetching happens.** The other direction occurs solely when you tick
«Create in Google Calendar as well» on an event — and then it is the event's
title, time and place. Meeting type, folder and language stay here.

Audio, transcripts, notes and documents never go that way.

## When it fetches

By itself **every fifteen minutes**, and when the app starts. If you have just
created something in Google and do not want to wait, press the sync icon in
the calendar's header. The line below says when it last fetched.

## Google Tasks

Tasks are a **separate** approval, kept apart from the calendar. It costs one
more login, and it is worth the price: someone who only wants to share their
calendar should not see «and your tasks» on Google's screen.

This one goes both ways: tasks you write in Google appear in the Cockpit, and
if you tick one off here, it is ticked off at Google.

**Tasks the app has found itself in a meeting are never sent up.** They come
from a transcript, and the content of a set of minutes should not end up in
the cloud because someone switched tasks on.

## Tasks from a meeting

A transcribed recording has a **Tasks** tab. There the app suggests tasks from
what was said, and you choose which ones become real.

Each task has a short name and a description. The short name is what appears
in the Cockpit; the description is the whole sentence somebody said.
