HeyPia Privacy Policy

Effective 28 August 2026

In short

  • The audio from your meetings never leaves your machine.
  • Recordings, notes and transcripts are kept on your own computer, not on a server of ours.
  • If you set up a key for summaries, the text of the transcript is sent to the provider — never the meeting audio.
  • When you dictate, that one clip is sent — and only while you hold the key down.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, your appointments are read so a recording can be tied to the right meeting. None of it is sold or shared.

What information the app handles

What you create in the app

  • Audio recordings of your meetings — both the microphone and the sound from the speaker, so online meetings are included.
  • Transcripts — the written-out text of the recording.
  • Your notes, written during or after the meeting.
  • Documents the app produces: summaries, minutes and tasks.
  • Settings and a word list that improves from the corrections you make yourself.

What is fetched from Google, if you connect your account

Connecting is optional. The app works without it; the calendar tab simply reads «not enabled».

Scope What it gives access to What the app uses it for
calendar.events The events in your calendar — reading them, and creating and editing them Shows today's meetings so a recording can be tied to the right event. Creates or edits an event when you ask for it in the app.
tasks Your task lists in Google Tasks Shows your open tasks in the Cockpit and uploads the tasks you yourself select from a meeting.

The app does not read your mail, your files in Google Drive, your contacts or your photos, and does not ask for access to them.

Where the information is kept

Everything you create in the app is stored in a folder on your own computer — by default C:\AppNoter. There is no account, no server and no synchronisation we can read along in.

The information fetched from Google is held only in the app while it runs, and in the local folder if it belongs to a recording. The key to your Google account is stored locally on the machine.

What leaves the machine

Meeting audio stays where it is

A meeting recording is transcribed to text on your own machine. The audio file is never sent anywhere, whatever other features you switch on. If anything from a meeting is sent onward, it is the transcript and nothing else.

The reason is that a meeting has people in it who chose nothing themselves. Their voices are not ours to move.

Dictation is the one exception

When you dictate — hold the shortcut key down and speak — that one clip is sent to the provider to be written out. It is your own voice, your own seconds, and it happens only while you hold the key yourself.

Nothing changes for the people in the meeting. Their audio stays on the machine. The difference is who chose: you pressed, they did not.

Dictation can be switched off under Settings → Dictation. With it off, no audio leaves the machine at all.

If the app is to produce a summary, minutes or tasks, that happens at a provider, and the text of the transcript is sent there. It is optional: without a key the app can only transcribe, and nothing leaves the machine.

The provider is Mistral AI, and the app deliberately uses their European endpoint (api.eu.mistral.ai) so processing takes place within the EU. Every single transmission is logged in the app with time, model, character count and price, so you can see exactly what was sent.

The app also fetches updates and language models from the internet. Those requests contain nothing about you or your meetings.

Google user data: limited use

HeyPia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Concretely, this means:

  • The information is used only to provide the features you see in the app — the calendar and the tasks.
  • It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • It is not transferred to others, except when you ask for it yourself or the law requires it.
  • It is not used to train language models — neither ours nor anyone else's.
  • No humans read it, apart from you.

How long it is kept

For as long as you keep it yourself. The files are yours and sit on your machine. If you delete a recording in the app, or delete the folder, it is gone — there is no copy at our end.

How to withdraw access

  • In the app: Settings → disconnect from Google.
  • At Google: at myaccount.google.com/permissions you can remove HeyPia's access regardless of what the app does.

Access can be withdrawn at any time, and the rest of the app carries on without it.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right of access to, rectification of and erasure of your personal data, and the right to object to the processing.

In practice you exercise them yourself: the information sits in a folder on your own computer, which you can open, change and delete without asking anyone. If you still have a question, write to jorgen@vagtsom.com.

You may lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Recording meetings is your responsibility

The app reminds you to tell the participants that a recording is being made, and that prompt appears every time on purpose. The rules differ from country to country, and it is you who is recording — not us. Say it out loud before you press.

Changes to this policy

If it changes, the date at the top of the page changes. If the change is material, it is said in the app as well — not only here, where nobody looks.
